Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to record or edit anything?

No. You give a topic and get a finished video with voice-over and captions.

What can I do on the free plan?

You get 6 credits to try it, which covers about three 30-second videos, up to 2 videos a day.

Which formats do you support?

Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, plus 16:9, 1:1 and 4:3.

Where does the footage come from?

Stock footage is matched to each line of the script automatically.

Can I upgrade or change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade from Settings inside the app at any time.
